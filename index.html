Threat Alerts

Stay aware of active fraud patterns

Fake KYC / Account Block SMS

Fraudsters send messages claiming your account will be blocked unless you complete KYC through a link. Real banks never do this.

UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammers send collect requests pretending you will receive money. Approving the request actually sends money from your account.

Fake Job / Refund Links

Links promising refunds or jobs often lead to phishing pages designed to steal OTPs and passwords.

Screen Sharing / Malicious APK

Victims are tricked into installing unknown APKs or sharing screen, giving fraudsters full device control.
