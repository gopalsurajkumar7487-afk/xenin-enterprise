Background Protection

Important Limitation

Modern Android does not allow normal apps to fully monitor and block all SMS, UPI payments, or banking activity in the background. This is a system-level restriction for user privacy and security.

What Xenin AI can do:

  • • Manual scanning of SMS, UPI, links and QR codes
  • • AI guidance when you ask
  • • Local scan history
  • • Emergency helpline information

What it cannot do:

  • • Automatically block bank transactions
  • • Silently read all your SMS without restriction
  • • Stop UPI payments in real time
  • • Act as full antivirus or bank firewall
Xenin AI is a protection assistant, not a real-time transaction blocker. Always verify important payments yourself and report fraud on 1930.
