Threat Alerts

Current fraud patterns you should know

Fake KYC / Account Block SMS

Fraudsters send messages claiming your bank account will be blocked unless you complete KYC through a link. Real banks never request KYC via random SMS links.

UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammers send collect requests pretending you will receive money. Approving the request actually sends money from your account.

Fake Job / Refund Links

Links promising refunds, job offers or lottery winnings often lead to phishing pages designed to steal OTPs and passwords.

Screen Sharing / Malicious APK

Victims are tricked into installing unknown APKs or sharing their screen. This can give fraudsters full control of the device.
