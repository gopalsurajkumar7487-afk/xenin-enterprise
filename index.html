Fraud Alerts

Common active fraud patterns (awareness only)

Fake KYC / Account Block SMS

Fraudsters send SMS saying your bank account will be blocked unless you complete KYC via a link. Real banks never ask for KYC through random SMS links.

UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammers send collect requests pretending you need to “receive” money. Approving the request actually sends money from your account.

Fake Job / Refund Links

Links promising refunds, job offers or lottery winnings often lead to phishing pages designed to steal OTPs and passwords.

APK / Screen Sharing Fraud

Victims are tricked into installing APKs or sharing screen via apps like AnyDesk. This gives fraudsters full control of the phone.
