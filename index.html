Fraud Alerts

Common active fraud patterns

Fake KYC / Account Block SMS

Fraudsters send messages claiming your account will be blocked unless you complete KYC through a link. Real banks never request KYC via random SMS links.

UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammers send collect requests pretending you will receive money. Approving the request actually transfers money from your account.

Fake Refund / Job Links

Links promising refunds, job offers or prizes often lead to phishing pages designed to steal OTPs and login credentials.

Screen Sharing / Fake APK Fraud

Victims are tricked into installing unknown APKs or sharing their screen. This can give fraudsters full access to the device.
